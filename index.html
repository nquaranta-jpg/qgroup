About Q Group Brokers

A BROKERAGE BUILT ON STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Q Group Brokers is a commercial finance brokerage. We are not a lender — we work on your behalf to place your financing request with funding partners in our network, which includes our affiliate, Crown Merchant Financial. Every offer we present tells you who the funder is and how they're connected to us.

We arrange business-purpose financing only, and we'd rather tell you "not yet" than put you in capital that doesn't fit. No pressure, no surprises — just clear options and honest guidance.

Financing We Arrange

BUSINESS FINANCING

WORKING CAPITAL

Short-term capital for inventory, payroll, marketing, or seasonal gaps — placed with funding partners who can move in days, not months.

REVENUE-BASED FINANCING

Financing repaid as a share of your sales. Before you sign anything, we walk you through the full cost and how repayment actually works.

TERM LOANS & CREDIT LINES

Longer-term loans and revolving lines of credit for established businesses that want room to grow on their own schedule.

How It Works

THREE STEPS. NO OBLIGATION.

01

TELL US ABOUT YOUR BUSINESS

A short conversation: time in business, monthly revenue, and what the capital is for. No cost and no commitment to start.

02

WE SHOP YOUR REQUEST

We present your request to the funding partners in our network that fit your profile — including affiliated funders, which we always disclose.

03

YOU COMPARE & CHOOSE

You see each offer side by side — funder, amount, cost, and terms — and decide what's right for your business. Or walk away.

All approvals, amounts, rates, and terms are determined by the funding partner. We arrange financing; we don't guarantee it.

DISCLOSURES

Q Group Brokers is a commercial finance brokerage, not a lender or funder. We arrange financing for business purposes only; we do not arrange loans for personal, family, or household use. All financing is provided by third-party funding partners.

Q Group Brokers is affiliated with Crown Merchant Financial. Your financing request may be presented to affiliated funding partners; any such affiliation is disclosed with each offer. Q Group Brokers may receive compensation from funding partners in connection with financing we arrange, and this compensation may vary by partner and product.
