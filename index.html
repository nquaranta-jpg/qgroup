About Q Group Brokers

A BROKERAGE BUILT ON STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Q Group Brokers is a hard money loan brokerage. We are not a lender — we work on your behalf to place your loan request with private and hard money lenders in our network, which includes our affiliate, Crown Merchant Financial. Every term sheet we present tells you who the lender is and how they're connected to us.

We arrange business-purpose loans secured by investment real estate only — no owner-occupied or consumer loans — and we'd rather tell you "not this deal" than put you in a loan that doesn't pencil. No pressure, no surprises — just clear terms and honest guidance.

How It Works

THREE STEPS. NO OBLIGATION.

01

TELL US ABOUT YOUR DEAL

A short conversation: the property, purchase price, rehab budget, after-repair value, and your timeline. No cost and no commitment to start.

02

WE SHOP YOUR LOAN

We present your deal to the private lenders in our network that fit the property and your track record — including affiliated lenders, which we always disclose.

03

YOU COMPARE & CLOSE

You see each term sheet side by side — lender, leverage, rate, points, and term — and decide what's right for your deal. Or walk away.

All approvals, amounts, rates, and terms are determined by the lender. We arrange financing; we don't guarantee it.

DISCLOSURES

Q Group Brokers is a hard money loan brokerage, not a lender. We arrange loans for business purposes only, secured by non-owner-occupied investment real estate; we do not arrange loans for personal, family, or household use, or loans secured by a borrower's primary residence. All financing is provided by third-party lenders.

Q Group Brokers is affiliated with Crown Merchant Financial. Your loan request may be presented to affiliated lenders; any such affiliation is disclosed with each offer. Q Group Brokers may receive compensation from lenders in connection with financing we arrange, and this compensation may vary by lender and product.

All approvals, amounts, rates, fees, and terms are determined solely by the lender and are not guaranteed. Products and services are not available in all states. Nothing on this site is an offer or commitment to lend, or financial, legal, or tax advice.
